Privacy Policy

hiroKapp / Keita Hirose

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Keita Hirose ("we", "us", or "our") provides mobile apps under the hiroKapp name. This policy explains how information is handled when you use our apps and contact us for support.

This is a common policy for our apps on iOS and Android. Available features and data handling vary by app, platform, and version. Please also read the App Privacy or Data safety information on the relevant app's store listing and any feature-specific notices shown in the app. A service described below is not necessarily used by every app.

1. Information We Handle

  • Data stored on your device. Apps may store game progress, preferences, and save data locally to provide their features. Local storage alone does not send that data to us. Device or operating-system backup settings may create additional copies.
  • Information you send to support. If you contact us, we receive the information you submit, such as your email address, message, device model, operating-system version, app version, and any attachments you choose to provide.
  • Technical and usage information. Apps that use advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or platform services may allow those services to process information such as IP address, device and operating-system details, app interactions, advertising identifiers where permitted, and error or diagnostic information. The data involved depends on the services enabled in the specific app and version.
  • Account or cloud features. Where an app offers an account or cloud-save feature and you choose to use it, the feature may process account identifiers, email address, and the data you choose to synchronize. Check the notice for that feature before enabling it.

Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, or other sensitive information in a support inquiry.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used as applicable to provide app features, save or restore progress, respond to inquiries, investigate bugs, maintain security, and improve reliability. Where advertising or analytics services are enabled, information may also be used to display and measure ads or understand app usage, subject to applicable permissions and the app's disclosures.

When consent or a device permission is required, the relevant feature must obtain it before the corresponding processing begins. You can change available permissions in your device settings. Contact us about withdrawing consent for information you have submitted directly.

3. Service Providers and Sharing

Depending on the app and version, third-party services may include Google Play services, AdMob, and Firebase Analytics. Support forms, website hosting, and any enabled cloud features may also be operated by service providers. These services are subject to their applicable terms and privacy policies. The inclusion of a service in this common policy does not mean it is integrated into every app.

Service providers may process information needed to deliver their services. We require providers handling personal information on our behalf to protect it and use it only for the relevant service. We do not sell personal information you send directly to us for support.

Information may also be disclosed where legally required or necessary to protect users, our services, or legal rights. Providers may process information in countries other than your country of residence, subject to applicable data-protection requirements.

4. Retention and Deletion

Local app data remains on your device until it is removed through an available reset option, the operating system's app-data controls, or deletion of the app and its data. Backups and files you export may remain separately and must be managed through the relevant device, account, or storage service.

We retain support correspondence only as long as needed to address the inquiry, resolve related issues, or meet legal obligations. You can request access, correction, or deletion of information you have sent us through the support contact below. We may need to verify the request and may retain information where required by law.

For enabled account or cloud features, use the deletion controls provided by the app or service, or contact us for assistance. Data held by independent platform or advertising services is also subject to those services' retention rules and account or device controls.

5. Websites, Support Pages, and Cookies

This privacy-policy page does not include advertising or analytics scripts. When you visit a hosted page or submit a support form, the hosting or form provider may process connection information, such as your IP address and browser details, and may use cookies or similar technologies to operate its service.

Device app storage and browser cookies are different technologies. Where cookies or similar technologies are used by an app or a service it opens, the available browser, device, and service controls apply.

6. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information we handle. No method of internet transmission or electronic storage is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

7. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly request personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information to us without the required consent, please contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action, including deletion where applicable.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when our apps, services, or information-handling practices change. The updated policy and its revision date will be posted on this page. Where required, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent before a material change takes effect.
